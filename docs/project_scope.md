# Project Scope

## 1. Project Title
Exploring Hydro-climatic Drivers of Flood Events Using Open Data

## 2. Research Question
How do extreme rainfall characteristics and antecedent wetness conditions influence the timing and magnitude of flood events at the catchment scale using open reanalysis data?

## 3. Motivation
Flooding is a recurrent hazard in many data-scarce regions, where limited hydrometric observations hinder detailed process-based analysis. Open reanalysis products provide an opportunity to explore hydro-climatic drivers of flood events in such regions using reproducible and transferable methods.

This project aims to build a transparent, open-data workflow to explore rainfall–flood relationships at the catchment scale, serving as a foundation for further MSc and PhD-level research.

## 4. Study Area
The study focuses on the Nyamwamba River catchment in Uganda, a basin characterized by high rainfall variability and frequent flood events. The catchment represents a typical data-scarce tropical basin where flood impacts are significant and observational data availability is limited.

## 5. Data Philosophy
This project strictly uses:
- Open-access data
- Reproducible workflows
- Script-based analysis (no manual processing)

Primary climate data will be derived from ERA5 reanalysis products. Hydrological response will be characterized using either observed discharge data (if available) or rainfall-based flood proxies.

## 6. Methodological Scope
The analysis is exploratory and focuses on:
- Identification of extreme rainfall events
- Computation of antecedent precipitation indices
- Temporal linkage between rainfall conditions and flood indicators
- Sensitivity of results to threshold and window choices

The project does not aim to provide operational flood forecasts or calibrated hydrological model outputs.

## 7. Limitations
- Reanalysis precipitation may not fully capture local-scale rainfall extremes.
- Flood indicators may rely on proxy-based definitions in the absence of discharge data.
- Results are basin-specific and not intended for direct generalization.

These limitations are explicitly acknowledged and documented.

## 8. Expected Outcomes
- A reproducible Python-based workflow for hydro-climatic event analysis
- Event-level datasets linking rainfall characteristics and flood indicators
- Clear visualizations supporting process interpretation
- A PhD-application-ready research portfolio component

## 9. Status
This project is self-initiated and has not been peer-reviewed.
